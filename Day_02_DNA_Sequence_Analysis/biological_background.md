# 🧬 Biological Background

DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) stores the genetic information of all living organisms.

DNA consists of four nucleotides:

- Adenine (A)
- Thymine (T)
- Guanine (G)
- Cytosine (C)

The order of these nucleotides forms the genetic code.

One of the most important measurements in genomics is the **GC content**, which represents the percentage of Guanine (G) and Cytosine (C) in a DNA sequence.

GC content influences:

- Genome stability
- Evolutionary relationships
- PCR primer design
- Gene prediction
- Comparative genomics

Another important concept is **transcription**, where DNA is converted into RNA by replacing Thymine (T) with Uracil (U).

Understanding these basic sequence properties is an essential first step before more advanced bioinformatics analyses.
